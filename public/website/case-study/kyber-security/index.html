Client

Industry

Home Repair

Service

Date

Overview

Kyber Home Services is a local contracting business that was losing potential jobs due to missed calls while on the field.

When they approached us, they were struggling with inefficient lead management and lacked a system to immediately text back missed calls.

Their existing tools were fragmented. They needed a single solution that could automate follow-ups, capture reviews, and handle scheduling automatically.

Challenges

A comprehensive content writing workflow can be a total fit. We follow flexible paths for discovery, a collaborative process that supports full-cycle.

Frameworks of brand adapted. They do a brilliant blend of brand expression and operation design that supports full-cycle.

They truly understood our local business needs and helped us stop losing jobs to our competitors.

Sarah Mitchell

Owner

Solution

By implementing the Missed Call Auto-Responder and Unified Inbox, we transformed how they handled incoming leads.

  • Set up automated SMS text-back for missed calls
  • Centralized all Facebook and Website messages
  • Automatic review requests after job completion
  • 24/7 web scheduling for estimates

Results

Within just 30 days, their booking rate skyrocketed simply because no lead was left hanging.

The automated workflows saved the owner over 10 hours a week in phone-tag and admin work.
